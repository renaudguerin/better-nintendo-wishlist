Settings

Privacy

This extension has no telemetry and no background processing.

It reads wishlist and catalogue information from Nintendo to display your wishlist. On US pages, it reads existing Nintendo sign-in/session information from the Nintendo page only to authenticate wishlist requests sent to Nintendo; it does not copy or persist that information in extension settings or send it to the developer. On supported Europe and US pages, when you confirm a removal, it sends that wishlist removal to Nintendo. When you choose a Review search, the item title and platform are sent to Google Search. Choosing the project link below navigates to GitHub.
