Better Wishlist for Nintendo eShop

Choose what appears in your wishlist and when a sale counts as ending soon.

Settings

Privacy

The extension has no analytics, telemetry, or background worker. Your settings stay in your browser.

It reads wishlist and catalogue information from Nintendo to display your games. On both supported storefronts, it uses the Nintendo sign-in session already open in the page to authenticate requests to Nintendo. It does not save that session in the extension or send it to the developer.

When you confirm a removal, the extension sends the request to Nintendo. Choosing Search reviews opens Google Search with the game title and, when known, the platform. The project link below opens GitHub.
