Better Wishlist for Nintendo eShop

Find games faster and catch sales before they end.

A free extension for your existing Nintendo eShop wishlist, built for Chrome and desktop Firefox.

Desktop Firefox 140 or later is required.

Find your next game

Search by name and filter by sale, free demo, platform, or content type. Combine Free Demo and On Sale to find discounted games you can try first.

Keep track of deals

Sort by sales ending soon, price, discount, release date, or when you added a game. Search online reviews from a game card.

Make room for more

Select games you no longer want and confirm their removal together. The responsive layout and dark mode make larger lists easier to browse.

Compatibility and privacy

Use your Nintendo account’s existing wishlist on the European eShop, the US site, or Americas storefronts such as Mexico, French Canada, and Brazil. Other Americas locales are supported on a best-effort basis. From Europe, you can also choose to view unavailable items from other regions.

Nintendo supplies your wishlist and catalogue data. Your settings stay in your browser, and the developer receives no wishlist data. When you choose to search for reviews, Google Search opens with the game title and, when known, its platform. Read the privacy policy.
