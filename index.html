Better Wishlist for Nintendo eShop

The wishlist tools you wish the eShop had!

Search, filter, sort your wishlisted games, and catch sales ending soon.

GitHub page Bugs & suggestions

Available for Chrome and desktop Firefox 140 or later.

Nintendo’s wishlist page is one long list with no way through it. Finding anything means scrolling, and spotting a sale before it ends is pure luck.

Third party wishlist sites require maintaining a separate list. You can't wishlist new games from your Switch and expect them to sync there.

This extension fixes all of that, right on Nintendo’s website, using your existing Nintendo wishlist: no separate account, nothing to set up or sync.

Cluttered wishlist?

Load it all in one go, then search by name with results narrowing as you type. Filter by current sales, free demo availability, platform (Switch or Switch 2), or content type (games, DLC, and bundles like Deluxe editions). Combine Free Demo with On Sale to find discounted games you can try first. Once you’ve picked what to drop, remove all selected items in two clicks.

In the mood for new games?

Sort by sales ending soonest, price, discount, newest release, or even oldest or newest on your list. Not sure about a title? Search online reviews in one click.

Browsing late at night, on a big screen?

Dark mode and a responsive layout: see more of your list at once, two or three columns side by side, easy on your eyes.

Wishlists around the world?

Works with the European eShop (ec.nintendo.com/my/wishlist), the US wish list (nintendo.com/us/wish-list), and Americas storefronts such as Mexico, French Canada, and Brazil. Other Americas locales are supported on a best-effort basis. If your wishlist mixes games from different store regions, you can optionally view unavailable items together from the European site.

Privacy

The extension accesses your wishlist through the Nintendo sign-in already open in the page. Wishlist requests go to Nintendo, not the developer. Your settings stay in your browser. Google Search opens with the game title and, when known, platform only when you click “Search reviews”.
