Better Wishlist for Nintendo eShop

The wishlist tools you wish the eShop had! Search, filter, sort your games, and catch sales before they end.

Source on GitHub Report a bug

Nintendo’s wishlist page is one long list with no way through it. Finding anything means scrolling, and spotting a sale before it ends is pure luck.

This extension fixes that, right on Nintendo’s own site, using your existing Nintendo sign-in: no separate account, nothing to set up.

Too many games, too little time?

Load them all in one go, then search by name with results narrowing as you type. Filter by current sales, platform (Switch or Switch 2), and content type (games, DLC, bundles like Deluxe editions). Once you’ve picked what to drop, remove several items in two clicks.

In the mood for a spree?

Sort by sales ending soonest, price, discount, newest release, or even oldest or newest on your list. Not sure about a title? Search online reviews in one click.

Browsing late at night, on a big screen?

Dark mode and a responsive layout: see more of your list at once, two or three columns side by side, easy on your eyes.

Wishlists around the world?

Works with both the European eShop (ec.nintendo.com/my/wishlist) and the newer US wish list (nintendo.com/us/wish-list). Changed your Nintendo account region at some point? If your wishlist mixes games from different store regions, you can optionally view them all in one place, from the EU site.

Privacy, briefly

The extension reads your wishlist through the Nintendo sign-in already open in the page, exactly like the eShop’s original wishlist page. None of your data goes to the developer or any third party. Your settings stay in your browser. Google Search opens only when you click “Search reviews”.
