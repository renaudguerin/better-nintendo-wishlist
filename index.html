Better Wishlist for Nintendo eShop

Search, filter, and sort your Nintendo eShop wishlist — and catch the sales before they end.

Preparing for the Chrome Web Store

Nintendo’s wishlist page gives you one long list and no way through it. Once you’ve saved a few dozen games, finding anything — or noticing that something you wanted is on sale and about to go back up — means scrolling and squinting.

This extension adds a proper management view to that page, on Nintendo’s own site. Nothing to sign into, nothing to sync, no second wishlist to maintain.

What you get

  • Search by game title
  • Filters for sale, platform, and content type — games, DLC, or bundles
  • Sorting by name, price, discount, release date, date added, or sale ending soonest, remembered per store
  • Sale-ending warnings, with a window you choose
  • Removal of one game or several at once, always after an explicit confirmation
  • Light, dark, or system theme

Privacy

No analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, no background process, no remote code, and no server operated by the developer. Your settings stay in your browser. One permission, storage, and it runs only on the two addresses above.
