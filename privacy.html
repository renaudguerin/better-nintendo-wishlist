Privacy Policy

Better Wishlist for Nintendo eShop · Effective 11 August 2026

This extension collects nothing about you. It has no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, no background process, no remote code, and no server operated by the developer.

There is no developer-operated server. Nothing you do in this extension is sent to the developer, because there is nowhere for it to be sent. Your settings never leave your browser.

What the extension does with your data

Nintendo

On supported Nintendo wishlist pages, the extension reads your wishlist and the related catalogue information from Nintendo so it can display your list with search, filters, and sorting.

On US pages, it reads the sign-in session that already exists in the Nintendo page, and uses it only to authenticate wishlist requests sent to Nintendo. It does not copy that session information into extension settings, store it, or transmit it anywhere else.

When you confirm a removal, the extension sends that removal to Nintendo, in the same way the Nintendo page itself would. Removals are never automatic and always require an explicit confirmation from you.

Google Search

If you choose the “Review” link on a game, a Google Search opens in a new tab containing that game’s title and, where known, its platform. This only happens when you click it.

GitHub

The options page links to this project’s public repository on GitHub. It is an ordinary link: nothing is sent unless you choose to go there.

Payments

There are none. This release contains no paid tier, no donation link, and no payment destination of any kind. The extension never requests, displays, or handles payment details. Every feature is free.

What is stored on your device

Everything the extension saves stays in your own browser.

  • Extension settings are stored in your browser’s extension storage as nwm_settings_v1_showCrossRegion, nwm_settings_v1_saleUrgencyDays, and nwm_settings_v1_theme.
  • Your filter and sort choices are stored per store, in that store’s own localStorage, under nwm_preferences. Because this storage is tied to the site, your European and US preferences stay separate.

These values are never transmitted anywhere. Removing the extension removes its settings.

Permissions

The extension requests a single permission, storage, which is what lets it remember your settings between visits. It runs only on the supported Nintendo wishlist addresses and has no access to any other site you visit. It has no background worker and does not download or execute code from the internet.

Third parties

Nintendo, Google, GitHub, and your browser each handle data under their own privacy policies when you use the relevant page, search, or link. This policy covers only the extension itself.

Children

The extension is a convenience layer over a page you are already signed in to. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the extension’s changelog.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or anything else, are best raised as an issue on the public project tracker.
